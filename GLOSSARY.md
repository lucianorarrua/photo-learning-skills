# Photo Learning

Shared vocabulary for guided learning in photo editing and RAW development.

## Language

**Lesson**:
A unit of learning with one concrete learning objective, practice on one photo, and the learner's explanation of the effects and trade-offs of their decisions. A photo can support multiple lessons, and a lesson can span multiple sessions; a finished edit alone is not evidence of learning.
_Avoid_: Session, finished edit

**Learning objective**:
A specific understanding or decision-making ability that the learner practices and demonstrates. The same learning objective can recur in separate lessons using different photos.
_Avoid_: Lesson, task to finish an edit

**Session**:
A working encounter between the learner and the agent that can start, continue, or complete a lesson.
_Avoid_: Lesson

**Demonstrated understanding**:
Provisional evidence of learning from the learner's recognition of an adjustment's effect and explanation of its trade-offs in their own words, including any prompting needed. It is specific to what was observed, rather than a global proficiency rating or self-reported experience.
_Avoid_: Skill level, self-reported experience

**Operation preference**:
The learner's revisable preference for who manipulates editing controls: the learner, the agent executing agreed experiments, or both taking turns. It does not remove the learner's role in predicting, observing, explaining, and deciding.
_Avoid_: Teaching pace, learning objective

**Photo collection root**:
The learner-selected directory containing a photo collection and its existing subdirectory organization. It is the shared working context for learning with that collection across agent clients.
_Avoid_: Album, learning objective

**Learning context**:
The learner's recorded preferences, provisional learning progress, and session history used to provide continuity. Each photo collection has its own learning context by default; collections can explicitly share one complete context.
_Avoid_: Chat history, photo collection
