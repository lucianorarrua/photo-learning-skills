## Language

When interacting with `lucianorarrua`, communicate in Spanish, including
questions, progress updates, explanations, and final responses. For other
users, follow their preferred language; this Spanish preference is personal
to `lucianorarrua`.

Write all project artifacts in English, including documentation, code,
comments, filenames, issues, pull requests, labels, and commit messages.
Keep technical identifiers and quoted source text in their original language.

isolated-worktree setup. For `/pr`, read `.agents/skills/pr/SKILL.md` and follow
